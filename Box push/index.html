Game instructions

Push the colored boxes to the corsponding colored destinations. Your goal is to push all the colored boxes to their destinations to comlete a level using as fewest moves as possible. You can only push one box at a time. Watch out for pits that can make the player and boxes fall down. High tiles blocks the player and boxes and slightly high tiles only blocks boxes. Boxes that are not green cannot go on green squares. Boxes will move on conveyor belts if there is a clear path. Box colors can be mixed when two boxes are pushed into flashing tiles.
Controls:

  • y: Zoom in
  • i: Zoom out
  • u, j, h, k: Rotate camera
  • w: Move up
  • a: Move left
  • s: Move down
  • d: Move right
